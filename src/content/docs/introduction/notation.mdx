Each letter represents a face, just like a normal 3x3 notation. We have the normal 3x3 faces (R, U, L, D, F, B), as well as two aditional moves for the extra faces (BR, BL)

<PuzzleViewer alg="R U L D F B BR BL BL' BR' B' F' D' L' U' R'" startState="solved" />

And two additional moves for the slices (Rs, Fs, Bs, Ls, Us, Ds, BRs, BLS).
