Each letter represents a face, just like a normal 3x3 notation. We have the normal 3x3 face moves (R, U, L, D, F, B), as well as two additional moves for the extra faces (BR, BL) on the back:

<PuzzleViewer alg="R U L D F B BR BL BL' BR' B' F' D' L' U' R'" startState="solved" />

And two additional moves for the slices (Rs, Fs, Bs, Ls, Us, Ds, BRs, BLs), a slice is like an M move on a 3x3, or a wide move followed by a counter move (Fs = f F'):

<PuzzleViewer alg="(r R') (f F') (b B') (l L') (u U') (d D') (br BR') (bl BL')" startState="solved" />

### Specific Notation

##  Sledge and Hedge

The algs pages use shorthand for the two common three-move triggers:

| Notation | Moves |
|----------|-------|
| **S** = sledge | `(R' L R L')` |
| **H** = hedge | `(R B' R' B)` |
| **S'** = inverse sledge | `(L R' L' R)` |
| **H'** = inverse hedge | `(B R B' R')` |

## Whole-puzzle rotations

| Notation | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Uo** | `{U, BR}` — rotate the puzzle so U stays on top and BR comes to front |
| **Uo'** | `{U, BL}` — rotate the puzzle so U stays on top and BL comes to front |
