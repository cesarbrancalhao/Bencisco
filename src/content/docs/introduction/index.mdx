Bencisco is the most used method for solving the Face-Turning Octahedron (FTO).
The workflow reduces the puzzle step-by-step, resembling the Yau reduction method used for larger cubes like the 4x4 and 5x5.

### The steps

1. **First Block**: Build one primary center intuitively using large and small trapezoid blocks.
2. **First Two Triples**: Form and solve two corner-triangle-triangle triples onto the initial face.
3. **Remaining 3 Centers**: Solve the next three adjacent centers while keeping the first face intact.
4. **Last Bottom Triple**: Solve and insert the final triple of the first layer/group.
6. **Last 3 Triples**: Orient the final triples using any of the standard methods to finish the last layer. The standard methods include 2LTCP, TCP, and 1LL3T.
