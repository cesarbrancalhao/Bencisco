- **Beginner** - First Block → Remaining 3 Centers → Pair Formation → 2LTCP. Core topics only; skip everything marked **Medium** and **Advanced**. The method is complete at 2LTCP.
- **Intermediate** - optimal solution tables, lookahead, deeper inspection.
- **Medium** - TCP. A manageable subset of 1LL3T.
- **Advanced** - OLP, 1LL3T, EO tracking, cancellations.

## Progress tracking

Cases on the alg pages have a **Mark as learned button** (graduation cap icon) in the top-right corner of each case card. Click it to mark a case as learned and store your progress in your browser. The sidebar badge for each set shows how many cases you've learned (e.g. _3/18_).

You can click the **Expand button** (diagonal arrows icon) in the top-left corner to open a detailed pop-up with the interactive puzzle viewer, scramble, all algorithms, and parity controls (OLP).
