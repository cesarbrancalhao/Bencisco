General FTO concepts only. Step-specific terms (OLP, TCP, 2LTCP, 1LL3T, 2FLIP, ...) are defined inline in their respective sections.

- **Corners** - are the three vertex/corner pieces of a face
- **Triangles** - are the four smaller adjacent triangular pieces flanking the corner
- **Triple** -  is a group of three connected pieces, a corner and two triangles on opposite sides
- **Edges** - are the slender, rhombus-ish shaped pieces between adjacent faces and corners
- **Centers** - are the hexagonal central regions of each face, they are formed of three edges and three triangles
