The Bencisco method was created by [Ben Streeter](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/persons/2013STRE01) in 2020. It is a portmanteau combining Ben (the first name of its creator, puzzle designer Ben Streeter) and Francisco (from a 3x3 method named Triangular Francisco).

Streeter has been an active member of the speedcubing community, contributing to methods and techniques for solving the Face-Turning Octahedron (FTO) and other puzzles.
