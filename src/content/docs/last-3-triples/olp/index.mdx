**OLP (One-Look Pair Formation)** completes pair formation in a single look, setting up [TCP](../tcp/). In these cases the triangles have symmetry about the vertical.

Two solutions are given per case, for even and odd permutation states respectively. For full TCP, choose the shortest. <strong class="text-yellow-500">Click the Even or Odd solution to swap the scramble preview.</strong>

The * marks the last flip and indicates where 2-flip avoidance can be used (simply swap between H and S). All algs are CIF.
