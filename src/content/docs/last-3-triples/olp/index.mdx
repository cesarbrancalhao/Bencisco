**OLP (One-Look Pair Formation)** completes pair formation in a single look, setting up [TCP](../tcp/). In these cases the triangles have symmetry about the vertical.

Two solutions are given per case, for even and odd permutation states, it is recommended to choose the shortest. <strong class="text-yellow-500">Click the Even or Odd solution to swap views.</strong> Odd solutions will always flip the pair parity, while even solutions will preserve it.

All algs are CIF. Specific notation on [Notation](../../introduction/notation).
